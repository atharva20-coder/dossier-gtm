Say This, Do This

Audio and screen together, top to bottom. The left column is what you do, the right is what you say while doing it.

READ

How to use this

Ten beats, roughly 6–7 minutes of talking plus however long the app actually takes. Expect six minutes end to end.

The amber blocks are the 8 real waits — a search running, a draft being written. Those are not dead air, they are where the reasoning goes. If a wait ends early, stop talking and move on; if it runs long, the next amber line is there to keep going.

One take, so decide this now

If you fluff a line, do not stop — pause for two seconds and say it again. A clean pause is one cut in the edit. Starting over costs you the whole run and a fresh set of search credits.

The one number you say out loud

Beat 5 says “41 results, and 27 were eligible”. That was the last run. Yours will differ — read what is actually on your screen and say those numbers instead. It is the only place the screen can contradict you.

Before you hit record

Persona set up and selected. Search budget checked — a company search plus one full research run is roughly 15 to 25 searches, and you have 697 left this month. Every unrelated tab and notification closed. Your .env nowhere near the screen. And do a dry run of the company search the day before, so you know it returns several people rather than one.

IF

If something breaks while recording

Say what happened and read the message off the screen. A tool that reports its own failure clearly is the point you have been making for six minutes. Do not apologise for it — use it, then carry on.

The one thing worth stopping for is a run that fails outright before beat 5, because everything after it depends on having a finished lead. Anything later, keep going and fix it in the edit.
